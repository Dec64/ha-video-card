## Known risks
- The profile copies video, so any HEVC or MPEG-2 channel will still fail on Fire tablets; the card names the codec (or shows "no picture" for MPEG-2), and a video-transcode profile fixes those channels.
- BBC One is 1080i (interlaced) H.264 High. Fire HD 8 hardware decode of interlaced 1080 is untested; T10 will show it.
- The HA connector points at Declan's live home HA (not a dev instance). Claude doesn't install or restart anything there; HA integration tests run against a disposable HA inside the Claude session instead.
- The HA iOS app may not support the feature mpegts.js needs (Managed Media Source); fallback planned.
- Ad-free YouTube in the embedded player is unlikely on iPhone (Safari blocks the needed cookies) and uncertain in Fully.

- **2026-10-23:** Flaky channel switching (seen with `buffer: stable`). Root cause: the stall watchdog's clock started when the engine loaded, not when data arrived. A cold Dispatcharr channel that took longer than the 8 s stall timeout to send its first byte was declared stalled the moment the data arrived (whenever the device hadn't appended it within one 500 ms tick, which a Fire tablet often doesn't), so the card dropped the connection, retried cold, and repeated until it gave up; a manual retry then worked because Dispatcharr had the channel warm. Both buffer modes had the bug; stable hit it more because its hold checks buffered growth, which lags the first data. Now the stall clock starts at the first data, and waiting for that is only the start timeout's job (`start_timeout`, 20 s). Also: a reconnect clears the Buffering label, and `setConfig` with a different `buffer` restarts the session (stop first, one connection) instead of keeping the old mode until the next channel. Fake server: new `slow-N` mode (first byte after N s); MSE shim: `__mseShimAppendMs` simulates a slow device's appends.
