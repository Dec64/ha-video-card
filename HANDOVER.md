## Open questions

## Known risks
- The profile copies video, so any HEVC or MPEG-2 channel will still fail on Fire tablets; the card names the codec (or shows "no picture" for MPEG-2), and a video-transcode profile fixes those channels.
- BBC One is 1080i (interlaced) H.264 High. Fire HD 8 hardware decode of interlaced 1080 is untested; T10 will show it.
- The HA connector points at Declan's live home HA (not a dev instance). Claude doesn't install or restart anything there; HA integration tests run against a disposable HA inside the Claude session instead.
- The HA iOS app may not support the feature mpegts.js needs (Managed Media Source); fallback planned.
- Ad-free YouTube in the embedded player is unlikely on iPhone (Safari blocks the needed cookies) and uncertain in Fully.

## Change log
- **2026-10-23:** Channel up/down after Declan's test of #11: a single press sat on Buffering for ever with `buffer: stable`. Cause: live TS buffers in pieces, and the browser stops at a hole in the buffer. `buffer: low` gets past holes through mpegts.js's catch-up jump; stable mode has none, and its reserve was measured only up to the first hole, so the hold could never end (and kept no stall going, since data still arrived). Now the reserve counts across holes up to 3 s, the session jumps the playhead over such holes (both modes), and a hold still short after 20 s while data arrives starts from the newest data. Also, channel up/down tunes once presses stop for 800 ms: every press releases the old stream at once and shows the new channel, but only the channel landed on opens a connection, and channels skipped past don't go into recents. MSE shim: new `__mseShimGap` knob. Initial load 37.4 KB gz.
- **2026-10-23:** Layout fixes from Declan's device testing of 2.0.0. Between about 520 and 630 px card width (wider with the 56 px wall-panel buttons, and zoom moves where that lands) the floating control bar ran off the right edge of the card, which clips it, so Channels and Full screen were unreachable. Full screen on an upright phone had the same problem with the exit button. The bar now drops the slider and separators and uses 44 px buttons under 680 px, wraps upwards if it still doesn't fit, and the phone layout's two rows are explicit. New `test/e2e/layout.spec.ts` (width sweep with mouse and touch sizes, full screen on phones) fails on the old CSS. Grey bar (Declan's screenshot of the FPL wall panel): the dashboard made the card ~160 px taller than its content and `ha-card` painted that as card background under the picture; `ha-card` and `.stage` are now flex columns and the picture takes any extra height (letterboxed, as in full screen). Stretched picture: Declan couldn't reproduce it; parked until he sees it again on a specific channel.
- **2026-10-23:** Settings panel (#10): sliders button in the control bar opens a glass panel with Buffering (Low delay / Stable), Now and next, Stats overlay and Reset. New `src/settings.ts` (`stream-card:settings:<target>`, `stream-card-settings` page event), `settings` config option and editor toggle, `buffer` in `debugInfo`. #11's buffer-change restart in `applyResolved` now compares against the device's mode (`bufferMode`), not the YAML. README and architecture contracts (card config, device memory) updated.
- **2026-10-23:** Flaky channel switching (seen with `buffer: stable`). Root cause: the stall watchdog's clock started when the engine loaded, not when data arrived. A cold Dispatcharr channel that took longer than the 8 s stall timeout to send its first byte was declared stalled the moment the data arrived (whenever the device hadn't appended it within one 500 ms tick, which a Fire tablet often doesn't), so the card dropped the connection, retried cold, and repeated until it gave up; a manual retry then worked because Dispatcharr had the channel warm. Both buffer modes had the bug; stable hit it more because its hold checks buffered growth, which lags the first data. Now the stall clock starts at the first data, and waiting for that is only the start timeout's job (`start_timeout`, 20 s). Also: a reconnect clears the Buffering label, and `setConfig` with a different `buffer` restarts the session (stop first, one connection) instead of keeping the old mode until the next channel. Fake server: new `slow-N` mode (first byte after N s); MSE shim: `__mseShimAppendMs` simulates a slow device's appends. Note that stable still costs ~8 s per switch on top of Dispatcharr's start-up, because Dispatcharr sends a new channel in real time.
